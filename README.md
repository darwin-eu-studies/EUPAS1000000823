# **DARWIN EU®  - Assessment of immunoglobulin use in clinical practice**

**DARWIN EU ID:** P4-C1-020

**HMA-EMA Study Catalogue:** [**EUPAS1000000823**](https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/node/4727/administrative-details)

**Results Explorer:** [**Web Application**](https://data.darwin-eu.org/EUPAS1000000823/)

**Study Description:**

Immunoglobulins can be routinely administered as replacement therapy in immunodeficient patients or are used as immunomodulating doses to treat various immune-related and inflammatory diseases. The global demand of immunoglobulins increases every year, resulting in intermittent shortages that could affect individuals with conditions that have limited alternative treatments. Prolonged low levels of certain immunoglobulins can increase the risk of certain infections and replenishment of these immunoglobulins can reduce this risk. This study aims to generate real-world evidence on the treatment and patient characteristics of individuals receiving immunoglobulins.
